Historical East Asian Trade Network, 1392–1450

Interactive Project

This project visualizes trade and diplomatic exchange networks between early Joseon Korea and Ming/Liaodong, Japan, Tsushima, Jurchen groups, and Ryukyu using records extracted from the Joseon Wangjo Sillok.

The interactive explorer will allow users to compare the Taejo–Jeongjong, Taejong, and Sejong periods, and distinguish overall trade, exports from Joseon, and imports into Joseon.
